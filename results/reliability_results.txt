Cryptocurrency Trade Anomaly Detection
Reliability Test

Test: Coinbase producer stopped and restarted.

Observation:
- Consumer remained available while waiting for messages.
- Coinbase producer was restarted.
- Consumer received and processed trades after restart.

Trades processed after restart: 635
Average throughput: 14.45 trades/sec
Average latency: 11646.38 ms
Maximum latency: 35287.52 ms

Limitation:
This test verifies producer restart recovery.
Kafka broker failure and automatic producer reconnection
have not yet been tested.
